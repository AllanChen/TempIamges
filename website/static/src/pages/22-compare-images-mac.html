如何开始对比

How to compare two images

  1. 打开两张图。在 Finder 中用 Glance 打开图片；如果已经打开一张，也可以将另一张拖进 Image Inspection 窗口。先确认两张图都出现在底部缩略图中。
  2. 切到并排视图。点击窗口工具栏的「对比」按钮。将原图与修改结果放在两侧，先查看整体构图与颜色。
  3. 切到滑杆视图。点击工具栏的「滑杆」按钮，拖动分界线，检查同一位置的边缘和局部变化。
  4. 放大细节。缩放并移动画面，检查文字、边缘、纹理和可能的生成瑕疵。对比后再决定是否使用处理结果。
  1. Open both images.Open images from Finder with Glance. If one is already open, drag the second into the Image Inspection window. Check that both appear in the filmstrip.
  2. Use side-by-side view.Choose Compare in the toolbar. Put the original and edited result in the two panes, then check the overall framing and color.
  3. Use the reveal slider.Choose Slider in the toolbar and drag the divider across the image to inspect edges and local changes at the same position.
  4. Zoom into details.Pan and zoom to check text, edges, textures, and possible generation artifacts before deciding whether to use the result.

哪种视图适合你的任务？

Which view should you use?

并排对比

Side by side

适合检查构图、尺寸比例、整体颜色和两种方案的差别。两张图保持可见，方便回看。

Best for framing, aspect ratio, overall color, and comparing two versions while keeping both visible.

滑杆检查

Reveal slider

适合在同一位置观察修图前后边缘、背景移除效果或细节变化。它是目视检查，不会计算像素差异。

Best for inspecting an edge, background removal, or a local edit at the same position. It is a visual check, not a pixel-difference calculation.

使用云端 Widget 生成结果时，Glance 可把原图与结果配成对比视图。原始文件不会因为查看对比而被改写。云端 Widget 的上传与点数规则见Widget 页面。

When a cloud Widget returns a result, Glance can pair it with the original for comparison. Viewing a comparison does not overwrite the source file. See AI Widgets for upload and credit rules.

常见问题

FAQ

不装软件，Mac 能并排对比两张图片吗？

可以粗略对比：用「预览」分别打开两张图，再把两个窗口用分屏（Split View）或窗口平铺放在屏幕左右两边。也可以在 Finder 里同时选中两张图后按空格，用快速查看浏览。系统自带方法没有滑杆，也不能在同一窗口里对齐检查同一位置。

并排和滑杆有什么区别？

并排适合看构图、比例和整体颜色；滑杆把两张图叠在一起，拖动分界线检查同一位置的边缘和局部变化，适合检查去背景、放大等 AI 处理结果。

Glance Preview 会自动找出两张图的像素差异吗？

不会。并排和滑杆都是目视检查，不计算像素差异。

图片对比要付费或登录吗？

不需要。图片对比是 Glance Preview 的免费查看功能，不需要账户或 Pro。只有云端 AI Widget 需要登录并消耗点数。

对比会改动原图吗？

不会。查看和对比不会改写原始文件。

Can I compare two images side by side on a Mac without extra apps?

Roughly, yes. Open each image in Preview and place the two windows next to each other with Split View or window tiling. You can also select both files in Finder and press Space to browse them in Quick Look. The built-in options have no reveal slider and cannot line up the same spot in one window.

What is the difference between side by side and a slider?

Side by side is best for framing, aspect ratio and overall color. A slider stacks the two images and lets you drag a divider to inspect edges and local changes at the same position, which suits checking AI results such as background removal or upscaling.

Does Glance Preview detect pixel differences automatically?

No. Side by side and the slider are visual checks; they do not calculate pixel differences.

Is image comparison free in Glance Preview?

Yes. Comparison is a free viewing feature that needs no account or Pro license. Only cloud AI Widgets need sign-in and credits.

Does comparing change my original file?

No. Viewing and comparing images does not overwrite the source file.
