终身更新与升级Lifetime updates and upgrades

Pro 是一次性买断，之后的所有功能更新都免费，没有续费。档位只决定同一账户可以同时激活几台 Mac。先买个人版、之后需要更多 Mac 时，可以在应用内按更高档位的全价升级，原授权和点数不受影响。

Pro is a one-time purchase and every future feature update is free, with nothing to renew. Tiers only set how many Macs one account can activate at the same time. If you start with Personal and later need more Macs, upgrade in the app at the higher tier's full price; your licence and credits are unaffected.

设备与离线使用Devices and offline use

个人版支持 1 个账号、1 台 Mac；多人版包含购买者共 5 个独立账号，每人 1 台 Mac，点数各自独立。换机时可以自行释放旧设备（每 30 天最多 3 次）。个人版离线最多 30 天，多人版最多 24 小时，联网后自动校验。

Personal includes one account and one Mac. Team includes the purchaser and four invited accounts, each with one Mac and separate credits. Release an old Mac yourself when you switch (up to 3 times every 30 days). Personal works offline for up to 30 days; Team for up to 24 hours and re-checks automatically when you reconnect.

付款方式Payment methods

付款由 Pancake by Waffo 作为记录商户（Merchant of Record）处理，并负责相关税费。人民币订单使用微信支付；美元订单可以用 Visa、Mastercard、Apple Pay、Google Pay 或微信支付。所有价格均为含税价。

Payments are processed by Pancake by Waffo as Merchant of Record, which also handles applicable taxes. CNY orders use WeChat Pay; USD orders accept Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay or WeChat Pay. Prices include tax.

退款Refunds

Pro 在付款后 14 天内可以全额退款。点数充值包在 14 天内且完全未使用时可以全额退款。系统原因失败的 Widget 任务会自动退回点数。详见退款政策。

Pro is fully refundable within 14 days of payment. Credit packs are fully refundable within 14 days if none of their credits have been used. Widget tasks that fail on our side refund credits automatically. See the Refund Policy.
