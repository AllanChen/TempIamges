在 Glance Preview 中选取并复制

Select and copy in Glance Preview

  1. 打开图片。在 Glance 的 Image Inspection 窗口查看含文字的图片，等待本机文字识别完成。
  2. 选中所需文字。在图片上拖选文字，按 ⌘C，然后粘贴到文档或其他 App。
  3. 需要完整结果时，从「工具」菜单选择「识别文字」。侧边结果窗口会显示这张图片识别出的全部文字，便于整体查看和复制。
  1. Open the image.View an image with text in Glance's Image Inspection window and allow local recognition to finish.
  2. Select what you need.Drag across the text in the image, press ⌘C, then paste it into a document or another app.
  3. For the full result,choose Tools → Recognize Text. The result panel shows the text recognized from the current image for review and copying.

Prefer a built-in option? Apple's Live Text guide for Preview explains how to select and copy text without installing Glance.

识别不准确时

When recognition misses text

先检查原图

Check the source image

模糊、小字、倾斜、反光和低对比度都可能影响结果。尽量使用清晰原图，而不是低分辨率截图。

Blur, tiny type, skew, glare, and low contrast can affect the result. Try a clear original rather than a small screenshot.

再查看完整结果

Review the full result

如果图片上的文字暂时无法选中，打开「工具 → 识别文字」查看是否已识别。菜单操作也可重试一次失败的扫描。

If text is not selectable yet, open Tools → Recognize Text to check the result. This action can also retry a failed scan.

Glance 目前优先使用系统支持的简体中文和英语识别。具体可识别语言取决于 macOS 的 Vision 支持情况；识别结果可能有错误，重要内容请对照原图核实。

Glance currently prefers Simplified Chinese and English when supported by macOS Vision. Available languages depend on the system, and recognition can make mistakes. Verify important text against the image.

常见问题

FAQ

在 Mac 的「预览」里怎么复制图片中的文字？

用「预览」打开图片，把指针移到文字上，等它变成文本光标后拖动选中，再按 ⌘C。这是 Apple 的「实况文本」，需要 macOS 12 Monterey 或更高版本。

「照片」App 里的图片也能复制文字吗？

可以。「实况文本」在「照片」和「快速查看」里同样可用：打开照片，拖动选中文字后按 ⌘C。

Glance Preview 识别文字时会上传图片吗？

不会。文字识别使用 macOS 的 Vision 框架在本机完成。只有你主动运行云端 AI Widget 时，所选图片才会上传。

在 Glance Preview 里复制图片文字要付费吗？

不用。这项功能免费，不需要 Pro、账户或点数。

为什么图片里的文字选不中？

可能是识别还没完成，或者文字模糊、太小、倾斜、对比度低导致识别失败。在 Glance Preview 里选择 Tools → Recognize Text 可以查看完整结果或重新识别；换一张更清晰的原图通常有帮助。

How do I copy text from an image in Preview on Mac?

Open the image in Preview, move the pointer over the text until it becomes a text cursor, drag to select, then press ⌘C. This is Apple's Live Text, available on macOS 12 Monterey or later.

Can I copy text from a photo in the Photos app on Mac?

Yes. Live Text also works in Photos and Quick Look: open the photo, drag across the text and press ⌘C.

Does Glance Preview upload my image to recognize text?

No. Text recognition runs on your Mac with the macOS Vision framework. An image is uploaded only when you run a cloud AI Widget.

Is copying text from images free in Glance Preview?

Yes. It needs no Pro license, no account and no credits.

Why can't I select the text in my image?

Recognition may still be running, or it may have failed on blurry, tiny, skewed or low-contrast text. In Glance Preview, choose Tools → Recognize Text to see the full result or retry the scan; a clearer original usually helps.
