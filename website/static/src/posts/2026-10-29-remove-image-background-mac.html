You open an image, and the background is wrong. macOS can remove it for free from Preview or Finder. If the edges come out rough, or you want to check the cut-out against the original in the same window, you can run a one-click AI tool from Glance Preview instead. The trade-off: the AI route uploads the image to the cloud and uses credits; the built-in route does neither.

Method 1: use what your Mac already has

In Preview. Open the image in Preview and click the Remove Background button in the toolbar, as described in Apple's Preview guide. If Preview offers to convert the file to PNG, choose File > Duplicate beforehand if you want to keep the original.

In Finder. Control-click the image, choose Quick Actions, then Remove Background (Apple's Quick Actions guide). If you don't see it, choose Customize to turn it on.

For a product on a plain background, this is often all you need. If the edges look rough, for example around hair, fur or glass, try the next method on the same image and compare.

Method 2: fix it from the preview in Glance Preview

  1. Open the image. In Finder, right-click the image and choose Open With > Glance. The Widgets menu is in the full image window, so if you start from Peek, open the image in the full window first.
  2. Run Background Remover Pro. Right-click the image and choose Widgets > Background Remover Pro. The menu shows the credit cost before you run it. Your initial cloud run asks you to sign in with Google or an email code. The tool runs in the background, so you can keep looking at other images. If the tool isn't in your Widgets menu yet, add it from the Widget Market (the Widget button in the toolbar).
  3. Compare with the original. The result opens next to the original. Click Slider and drag the divider, or click Compare to see both side by side. Zoom in on hair and fine edges before you keep the file.

The result is saved as a new file; your original is not changed.

Not happy with the cut-out?

Run the macOS route on the same image and compare the two results on the Slider. Running Background Remover Pro again costs credits again and may not give a different result. For hand-tuned edges, use an image editor with a mask or refine brush: Background Remover Pro is one click, with no brush and no text prompt.

Honest limits

  • Cloud and upload. Background Remover Pro uploads the image to Glance for processing and does not work offline. The AI model disclosure lists the model and the processing service.
  • Credits. Each run uses credits and needs an account. Viewing and comparing images do not. If a task fails on Glance's side, the credits are returned, as described on the Pricing page.
  • Purchases. New accounts start with 100 free credits, and you can buy more credit packs in the app (see Pricing).
  • Requirements. Apple silicon Macs, macOS 12.3 or later. The install page shows the one-time Open Anyway step.
  • Your images. Process images you own or have permission to edit. See the acceptable use policy.

After the cut-out

If the cut-out is clean but small, the Upscale Widget can raise the resolution. It redraws detail, so it can add detail that was not in the original: check it on the Slider. Both tools are listed on the AI Widgets page with their credit costs.

FAQ

How do I remove the background from an image on a Mac for free?

Recent versions of macOS can do it in Preview with the Remove Background button, or in Finder with Quick Actions > Remove Background. Neither costs anything.

Why is Remove Background greyed out in Preview on Mac?

On some macOS versions, file types or window sizes the Remove Background control in Preview can be missing or unavailable. Try Finder Quick Actions > Remove Background, duplicate or export the image as PNG and reopen it, or open the same file in Glance Preview and run Widgets > Background Remover Pro (20 credits), then check the cut-out with Compare or Slider.

How do I remove a background from the image I am previewing in Glance Preview?

Right-click the image, choose Widgets, then Background Remover Pro. The result appears next to the original, and you can check it with Compare or Slider.

Does Glance Preview's background remover cost money?

It uses credits: 20 per run at the time of writing, and new accounts start with 100. More credit packs can be bought in the app.

Does it upload my image?

Yes. Background Remover Pro runs in the cloud, so the image is uploaded to Glance for processing. Viewing and comparing images stay on your Mac.

Can I compare the cut-out with the original?

Yes. Click Compare for side by side or Slider to wipe between the two, and zoom in to check hair and edges before you keep the file.
