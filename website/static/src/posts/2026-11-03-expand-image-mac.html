You open a photo and the framing is tight: the subject sits too close to the edge, or you need more sky, floor or background for a layout. On a Mac you can crop or pad the canvas in Preview, but that does not invent new pixels beyond the edges. Expand Image in Glance Preview is a one-click cloud tool that extends the canvas with matching content, then lets you check the new edges against the original on a slider.

When Expand helps

Use Expand when you already like the subject but need more room around it: a tighter crop that leaves no margin for a slide, a product shot that needs a wider background, or a photo you want to reframe without cutting the subject. If you need a different aspect ratio with a fuller redraw of the whole frame, look at Redraw & Reframe on the AI Widgets page instead. If the image is simply too small, see how to upscale an image on Mac.

Expand from the preview in Glance Preview

  1. Open the image. In Finder, right-click the image and choose Open With > Glance. The Widgets menu is in the full image window, so if you start from Peek, open the image in the full window first.
  2. Run Expand Image. Right-click the image and choose Widgets > Expand Image. The menu shows the credit cost, 30 credits, before you run it. Your initial cloud run asks you to sign in with Google or an email code. The tool runs in the background, so you can keep looking at other images. If the tool isn't in your Widgets menu yet, add it from the Widget Market (the Widget button in the toolbar).
  3. Compare with the original. The result opens next to the original. Click Slider and drag the divider across the new edges, or click Compare to see both side by side. Zoom in on seams, faces, text and repeating patterns before you keep the file. You can also open both files later with the built-in image compare flow.

Your original file is not changed. Expand Image accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP and HEIC images. Per the AI model disclosure, it uses Black Forest Labs FLUX.2 and generates content beyond the input image boundaries. The live catalog summary is: "Extend the canvas beyond the edges with matching content."

Check the filled edges before you keep them

Outpainting invents pixels that were never in the file. That is useful for margins and backgrounds, and it is also why a quick wipe on the Slider matters. Look closely at:

  • Seams where the original meets the fill, especially along straight edges and horizons.
  • Faces, hands and logos near the border, which a model may alter as it extends the scene.
  • Text and repeating patterns, such as tiles, fences or fabric, which can drift or duplicate incorrectly.

If the fill looks wrong, keep the original and try another framing approach, or use a different Widget. Running Expand Image again costs credits again. This loop — preview, fix with a one-click tool, check — is the same pattern described in AI image preview on Mac.

Honest limits

  • Cloud and upload. Expand Image uploads the image to Glance for processing and does not work offline. The AI model disclosure lists the model and the processing service.
  • Credits. Each run uses 30 credits and needs an account. Viewing and comparing images do not. If a task fails on Glance's side, the credits are returned, as described on the Pricing page.
  • Purchases. New accounts start with 100 credits, and you can buy more credit packs in the app (see Pricing).
  • No direction or size panel confirmed. The public Widget catalog does not expose expand-amount or direction controls for this tool. Do not expect a separate panel for how far to expand; the run is one click.
  • Requirements. Apple silicon Macs, macOS 12.3 or later. The install page has the first-launch steps.
  • Your images. Process images you own or have permission to edit. See the acceptable use policy.

FAQ

How do I expand or outpaint an image on a Mac?

Preview can crop or pad the canvas, but it does not generate new scene content beyond the edges. In Glance Preview, open the image, right-click, choose Widgets > Expand Image (30 credits), then check the result with Compare or Slider.

How much does Expand Image cost in Glance Preview?

30 credits per run at the time of writing. New accounts start with 100 credits, and more credit packs can be bought in the app.

Does Expand Image upload my photo?

Yes. It runs in the cloud, so the image is uploaded to Glance for processing. Viewing and comparing images stay on your Mac.

Which AI model does Expand Image use?

Black Forest Labs FLUX.2, as listed on the AI model disclosure and Pricing pages.

Can I choose how far to expand or which direction?

The public Widget catalog for Expand Image does not list direction or size options. The run is one click; check the filled edges on the Slider before you keep the file.

Will the expanded area match the original exactly?

Not always. Generative fill invents content beyond the edges, so seams, text and fine patterns can look wrong. Compare against the original before you use the result.
