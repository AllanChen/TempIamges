An AI edit can look right at thumbnail size and still hide a soft edge, a warped letter or a changed face. Glance Preview is built for that last check: open the result next to the original, then use Compare or Slider before you decide to keep it. Comparing is free and happens on your Mac.

Why check at all

Cloud Widgets return a new image, and a new image can differ from the original in places you did not ask to change. Typical things to look for are edges after a background removal, text and small logos after an upscale, and the area around a removed object. A quick look at the pair is faster than finding the problem after you have sent the file on.

Step by step

  1. Run the Widget. Right-click the image, choose Widgets and pick a tool. Each Widget shows its credit cost before it runs. The result is saved as a new file, so your original stays as it was.
  2. Open the pair. Open the original and the result in Glance Preview. If one is already open, drag the other into the window. Check that both appear in the filmstrip. When a Widget returns a result, Glance Preview can pair it with the original for comparison.
  3. Start with Compare. Choose Compare in the toolbar to see the two side by side. Look at framing, proportions and overall color. This is the right view when the edit was meant to change the whole picture, such as an expanded canvas.
  4. Switch to Slider. Choose Slider and drag the divider across the image. At the same position you see the before and the after, which makes edge halos, missing detail and fill that does not match visible at once.
  5. Zoom into the risky spots. Pan and zoom to the places an AI edit usually gets wrong: hair, fingers, text, thin lines and the border of what changed.
  6. Decide. Keep the result, or run the Widget again; for Remove Objects, change the description of what to remove. Credits are charged when the server accepts a task, and the Widgets page lists the full refund rules for failed tasks.

What Compare does not do

Compare and Slider are visual checks. Glance Preview does not calculate pixel differences or flag changed areas for you, so zoom in and look. Viewing a comparison does not overwrite either file.

FAQ

How do I compare an AI edit with the original on a Mac?

Open both files in Glance Preview, choose Compare for a side-by-side view, then choose Slider and drag the divider across the image to inspect the same spot before and after.

Is comparing images free in Glance Preview?

Yes. Compare and Slider need no account or Pro license and run on your Mac without uploading the images.

Does Glance Preview find the differences automatically?

No. Both views are visual checks. It does not calculate pixel differences.

Does comparing change my original file?

No. Widget results are saved as new files, and viewing a comparison does not overwrite the source.

What if the AI result is not good enough?

Run the Widget again (for Remove Objects, with a different description of what to remove), or keep the original. Failed tasks are refunded automatically; see the Widgets page for the full rules.
