Widgets are the AI tools in Glance Preview: background removal, upscaling, expanding the canvas and more. You pick the ones you want from the Widget Market, and they appear in the right-click menu of the image window. Each Widget runs in the cloud and uses credits, and its cost is shown before you run it. This is part 2 of the Glance tutorial series; if you have not installed Glance yet, start with Getting started with Glance on Mac.

What you need

  • Glance Preview installed on an Apple silicon Mac (see the install page).
  • An internet connection: the Market loads its catalog from Glance's server.
  • A Glance account, but just when you run a Widget. Browsing and installing do not ask you to sign in.

Step 1: Open the Widget Market

Open any image in the full Glance window, then use one of these:

  • Click the Widget Market button in the window's toolbar (the grid icon).
  • Right-click the image and choose Widgets → Widget Market…. If no Widget is installed yet, Widget Market… sits at the top of the right-click menu instead.

The Market opens as a panel next to the window. The video window has the same Widget Market button.

Step 2: Find a Widget

  • Marketplace lists every Widget you can install, Installed lists the ones you have, and Updates lists installed Widgets with a newer version.
  • Use the search box to filter by name, or the sort button to sort by name.
  • If the list is empty or says it could not load widgets, click Refresh.

Step 3: Check the details, then install

Click a Widget to open its details page before you install it. It shows:

  • Cost per task, for example "20 credits / task", or "100 credits / 10 seconds" for video.
  • Commands: what the Widget does, with a short description of each command.
  • Input and Output: for example Image in, Image out, or Image in, Text out.
  • Privacy: how the Widget handles the file you send it.

Click Install. The Widget now appears under Widgets in the right-click menu of every image (or video) it accepts. To take it out of the menu again, open its details page and click Remove; when an update is available, the button reads Update.

Step 4: Run a Widget from the right-click menu

  1. In the full window, right-click the image and choose Widgets. Each entry shows its credit cost next to its name.
  2. Choose a Widget. On your initial cloud run, Glance asks you to sign in with Google or an email code. New accounts start with 100 credits.
  3. Some Widgets ask for an input before they run: Remove Objects takes a short English description of what to remove, Redraw & Reframe takes an aspect ratio, and Outfit Swap takes a second image with the outfit.
  4. The task runs in the background. Follow it in the Tasks window (the tray icon in the toolbar), and keep browsing other images meanwhile.
  5. When it finishes, the result is saved as a new file next to the original. Your original is not changed.

While a task is running on an image, the same right-click menu offers Cancel for it. Then check the result against the original with Compare or Slider, as shown in part 1.

What 100 starting credits cover

The 100 credits a new account starts with are enough for, for example:

  • 5 background removals (5 × 20), or
  • 3 upscales or expands (3 × 30 = 90), or
  • 2 background removals and 2 upscales (40 + 60).

A single 10-second video run uses all 100.

How credits work

  • Credits are just for cloud AI Widgets. Viewing, comparing and the local tools never use credits.
  • Charged per task. A Widget's cost is shown in the Market and in the right-click menu before you run it.
  • Not enough credits? Glance tells you how many more the task needs, charges nothing, and offers credit packs so you can top up inside the app. You can also top up any time from your account (Top up credits).
  • Buying credits. Credit packs are one-time payments with no subscription. Current packs and prices are on the Pricing page.
  • Failed tasks are refunded. If a task fails on Glance's side or times out in the queue, its credits are returned automatically.
  • Your balance is per account and shared across the Macs you sign in on. Credits do not expire.
  • Glance Pro does not include Widgets. Pro is a separate one-time purchase for local editing tools and comes with 100 bonus credits; Widgets keep using credits.

Good to know

  • Your file goes to the cloud. Running a Widget uploads the image or video to Glance for processing. Read each Widget's Privacy section in the Market.
  • Widgets can change. New Widgets show up in Marketplace, and prices can change; the Market shows the current list. If a Widget is withdrawn, it disappears from your menu.
  • Help. DM {{SUPPORT_X}} on X. A screenshot or the log file ~/Library/Application Support/Glance/app.log helps.

FAQ

Where is the Widget Market in Glance?

In the full image or video window: click the Widget Market button in the toolbar, or right-click the image and choose Widgets → Widget Market….

Do I need an account to install a Widget?

No. You can browse and install Widgets without signing in. Glance asks you to sign in with Google or an email code when you run a Widget for the initial time.

How many credits does a Glance Widget cost?

Image Widgets cost 20 to 30 credits per image: Background Remover Pro and Image to Prompt cost 20, Free Image Host 29, and Upscale, Expand Image, Redraw & Reframe, Remove Objects, Watermark Remover and Outfit Swap 30. Video Watermark Remover costs 100 credits per 10 seconds of video.

How many free credits do I get?

New accounts start with 100 credits, enough for five background removals or three upscales.

How do I get more credits?

Buy a credit pack inside Glance. Packs are one-time payments with no subscription, and the credits do not expire. Current prices are on the Pricing page.

What happens if a Widget task fails?

If a task fails on Glance's side or times out in the queue, the credits are returned automatically.

Do credits expire?

No. Credits stay on your account and are shared across the Macs you sign in on.
