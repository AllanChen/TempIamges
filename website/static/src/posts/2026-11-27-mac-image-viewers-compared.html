A folder test you can repeat in a minute

Put three numbered JPEGs in a folder. Open the middle file alone and press Right. Then select and open all three together and try again. At the last file, press Right once more. If you want to review every photo exactly once, stopping at the end matters. If you cycle through revisions with a client, returning to image 01 may be preferable. In Apple Preview, choose Settings → Images and set groups to open in the same window before the second pass.

FAQ

Can I flip through photos on a Mac without installing a viewer?

Yes. In Finder, select the photos, press Space and move through the selection with the arrow keys. Use Preview when you want the group to stay open while you annotate a file.

Which free viewer fits a folder of GIFs and still images?

qView is a good fit when you want to pause an animated GIF and browse stills in a minimal window. For a folder that also includes MP4 footage, choose Pixea.

Do I need Glance Pro to compare a retouched image?

No. Open the two images in Glance Preview and compare them side by side or with the slider for free. Credits apply when you run a cloud AI Widget.
