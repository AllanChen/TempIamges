Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

This policy applies to everything you upload to, generate with, or share from Glance cloud AI Widgets. It is part of the Terms of Service.

1. Prohibited content

You may not use Glance to create, edit, upload or share:

  1. Pornography and sexual content (NSFW): nudity, sexual acts or sexually explicit material, including “undressing” or nudifying any person.
  2. Violence and gore: graphic violence, gore, content that glorifies or incites violence, terrorism or self-harm.
  3. Hate speech: content that attacks or demeans people based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar characteristics.
  4. Child sexual abuse material and child-unsafe content: any sexual or sexualized depiction of minors, real or generated. We report such content to the relevant authorities.
  5. Deepfakes and impersonation: replacing or disguising one real person's face or identity with another's, compositing real people into fabricated intimate or compromising scenes, or making a real person appear to say or do something they did not. Glance offers no face-swap feature, and this applies whether or not the people involved consent.
  6. Copyright and trademark infringement: content that infringes others' intellectual property, including removing watermarks, logos or copyright marks from images or video you do not own or are not licensed to edit.

Also prohibited: any illegal activity, fraud or deception, harassment, and attempts to bypass credit charges, device limits, trials or other security features.

2. Photos of real people

Upload photos of real people only if they are of you, or you have the explicit permission of every person shown. Uploading another person's photos without consent is prohibited. Widgets such as Outfit Swap change clothing only and must not be used to sexualize, humiliate or misrepresent anyone.

3. Watermark removal

The watermark-removal Widgets are for content you own or are licensed to edit, for example removing your own draft watermark or a date stamp from your own photo. Using them on someone else's work is prohibited.

4. AI output disclosure

Results from cloud Widgets are generated or modified by AI. Do not present them as unedited originals where that could mislead people.

5. Models and moderation

The underlying AI models behind each Widget are listed on the AI Model Disclosure page. Glance has no text-to-image or image-to-video generation.

Current cloud Widgets work only on media you choose; none of them generates images or video from free-form text prompts. Before any Widget accepts free-form prompts, automated screening of prompts and outputs will be in place. We review reported content and results manually, and we can stop a Widget, cancel tasks and remove stored media and results at any time. Moderation records and decisions are kept so that they can be reviewed.

6. Reporting violations

Send a direct message to @Sir03834855 on X starting with “Report”, with a link or description of the content and, if you have one, the task or order number. We acknowledge reports within 2 business days and act on confirmed violations promptly. Reports of child sexual abuse material are handled first.

7. Enforcement

Depending on severity, we may remove content and results, cancel pending tasks, forfeit credits obtained through abuse, suspend Widget access, or suspend or permanently close the account. Serious violations, including child sexual abuse material, are reported to authorities. You can appeal a decision by sending a direct message to @Sir03834855 on X.
