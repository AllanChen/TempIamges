### 正文
命令敲到一半，只想瞄一眼原图。为了这一眼，还得切去 Finder 翻文件夹？

看录屏里的光标 👀 用 Glance，选中终端里的图片文件名，轻按一下 Option，图就弹在手边了。空格键都不用碰。

我喜欢的是看完这一步：认出是哪张图，就接着敲命令。真要找原文件，再点卡片上的「在 Finder 中显示」。

你在 Mac 上处理图片时，还有哪一步最想省掉？评论里说说，我挑一个录给你看。

## LinkedIn

### Post

Sometimes I’m halfway through a command when I need a quick look at a photo. The filename is already on screen. Opening Finder means following it to a folder, checking the image, then finding my place in Terminal again.

I wanted Glance to let me take that look right where I was working. In this recording, I select the image filename and tap Option. A preview opens beside the cursor. I can see the photo and its dimensions while the command stays in view.

The detail I like is where Finder fits in now. If all I needed was a peek, I can carry on typing. If I want to work with the original file, I can choose Show in Finder from the preview.
