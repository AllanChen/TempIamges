### 正文
做海报前，先挑一张合适的人像。有时照片选好了，背景却不适合接下来的排版。

Glance 是一个 Mac 看图软件。在里面看图时，可以右键打开 AI Widgets，选择「人物背景分离」，再查看抠图结果、对比处理前后。

这次视频演示的是海报准备里的这一步：看人像 → 右键去背景 → 对比结果。后续的海报排版需要另行完成。

视频为动画演示，界面为重绘示意，处理结果是示例效果，不是本次实际运行记录。实际效果以具体图片和处理结果为准。

Glance，看图顺手就改。

### Post
Before a portrait goes into a poster, there's a smaller task: getting the subject ready for the layout.

I'm building Glance, a Mac image viewer with AI Widgets available while you're looking at an image.

This short demo shows one step in that workflow: open a portrait, right-click to remove its background, then compare the cutout with the original. The poster layout comes afterward, in your design tool.

The aim is to make the transition from inspecting an image to preparing it feel more direct.

This is an animated demonstration with a redrawn interface and an example result, rather than a live test. Results vary by image.

Right-click. Glance. Done.
