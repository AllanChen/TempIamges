只改涂过的地方，包没了，其他画面保持不变，前后还能拉滑杆对比。

Fixing that usually means opening an editor, learning its masking tools, or uploading the photo to some website and hoping it doesn't change everything else.

I'm building Glance, a Mac app for viewing images. While looking at a photo, you can right-click → Widget → Edit Area, paint over the part you want changed, and describe it in plain words, like "Remove the bag."

Only the painted area is edited. Everything outside it stays as it was, and you can drag a slider to compare before and after.
