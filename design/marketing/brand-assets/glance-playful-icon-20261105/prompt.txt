Use case: logo-brand.
Create one original square macOS application icon concept, 1024 x 1024, for a desktop utility whose central job is instantly peeking at selected files, images and text in a floating preview window.

Metaphor: a preview window with a curious content card peeking out. Object + action + emotion: preview card + emerging for a quick look + alert curiosity.
One dominant compact object-character. A thick warm-white rounded rectangular window surround, near-frontal with a very slight three-quarter view, contains a dark navy recessed opening. A single warm-white rounded content card rises from inside the opening, its upper portion peeking above the window's upper-right edge. The card is behind the lower window rail and is visibly a flat broad card with rounded corners, not an animal or a robot. Two large simple navy pill-shaped eyes on the exposed card glance slightly sideways with curiosity. No mouth needed. The only signature hook is the card peeking over its own preview window. No extra props.
Build the entire silhouette from 4 broad rounded masses: window surround, peeking card, and two eye shapes. The silhouette occupies approximately 78% of the canvas width and 76% of height, centered with mild asymmetry; keep critical eyes and peeking-card contour within 12% safe margins. Large uninterrupted surfaces, generous negative space, recognizable window opening, clear overlap.

Visual lane: soft 3D object-character. One consistent satin soft-plastic material family, broad rounded bevels, shallow tactile depth. Broad soft light from upper left, restrained contact occlusion only where the card meets the window, one short soft shadow beneath the object. No dark cinematic lighting.
Palette: blue_trust reduced to three roles: saturated cobalt blue #1865EE full-bleed quiet background, warm soft white #F8F6F5 for window and card, ink navy #1C212C for eyes and the opening. Tonal shifts only to describe volume. High grayscale value separation between light subject and blue backdrop. Background reaches all four canvas edges. Square master artwork without a pre-rounded outer tile, no surrounding presentation board.
At 32 pixels the chunky window outline, raised card silhouette and two large eyes must remain legible. Avoid fine lines, tiny interface buttons, text lines or decorative details.

Negative constraints: no text, no letters, no wordmark, no watermark, no generic initial, no stars or sparkles, no rainbow, no magnifying glass, no animals, no arms or legs, no photographic textures, no hard chrome, no excessive gloss or glow, no busy scene, no additional icons, no border around the canvas. Do not imitate any existing app icon, logo, mascot, character, artist or studio. This must be an original product-specific visual.
