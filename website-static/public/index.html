常见问题

FAQ

需要登录吗？Do I need an account?

不需要。看图、看视频、对比和截图都不需要账户。只有第一次使用云端 AI Widget 或购买时，才需要用 Google 或邮箱验证码登录。

No. Viewing, compare and capture work without an account. You only sign in, with Google or an email code, the first time you run a cloud AI Widget or make a purchase.

我的文件会被上传吗？Are my files uploaded?

不会。查看和本地工具都在你的 Mac 上处理。只有当你主动运行一个云端 Widget 时，你选中的那张图片或那段视频才会上传处理。

No. Viewing and local tools run on your Mac. Only when you run a cloud Widget is the media you selected uploaded for processing.

Pro 是订阅吗？Is Pro a subscription?

不是。Pro 一次买断，已获得的版本永久可用，包含 12 个月功能更新。之后想要新功能，可以自愿续购一年更新，不会自动扣款。

No. Pro is a one-time purchase. The versions you get are yours to keep, with 12 months of feature updates included. After that you can optionally buy another year of updates; nothing renews automatically.

任务失败了会扣点吗？Am I charged if a task fails?

系统失败、排队超过 30 分钟、或在开始处理前取消，点数都会全额自动退回。任务开始处理后由你主动取消，点数不退。

If a task fails on our side, waits more than 30 minutes in the queue, or is cancelled before processing starts, its credits are refunded in full automatically. Cancelling after processing has started is not refunded.

试用结束后还能用什么？What still works after the trial?

查看图片和视频、对比、截图、图片文字识别与复制，以及按点数使用 AI Widget 继续免费。格式转换、压缩、导出、复制图片、编辑和 Base64 工具需要 Pro。你的文件、结果和点数都不受影响。

Viewing, compare, capture, image text recognition and copying, and credit-based AI Widgets stay free. Conversion, compression, export, image copying, editing and Base64 tools need Pro. Your files, results and credits are untouched.

怎么付款？How do I pay?

在应用内选择商品后，会打开 Pancake by Waffo 的安全收银台。人民币订单使用微信支付；美元订单可以用银行卡、Apple Pay、Google Pay 或微信支付。

After you choose a product in the app, Pancake by Waffo's secure checkout opens. CNY orders use WeChat Pay; USD orders accept cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay or WeChat Pay.
