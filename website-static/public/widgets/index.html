AI Widgets

右键，就地用 AI 改。

Right-click. Fix it with AI.

在 Glance 里右键图片或视频，选择 Widget 即可运行。结果出现在原图旁边，可以直接对比。下面的列表和价格实时读取自 Glance 服务。

Right-click an image or video in Glance and pick a Widget. The result appears next to the original so you can compare right away. The list and prices below are read live from the Glance service.

Failures on our side, queue waits over 30 minutes, and cancellations before processing starts are refunded in full; cancelling after processing starts is not. You are asked to confirm before submitting when a price is above the confirmation threshold or has changed.

Processing services and data

运行云端 Widget 时，你选中的媒体会上传到 Glance 服务，并交给下列服务处理。Glance 不会用你的素材训练模型。

When you run a cloud Widget, the media you selected is uploaded to the Glance service and processed by the services below. Glance does not use your media to train models.
