底层 AI 模型说明

Underlying AI models

Glance 没有文生图和图生视频功能。每个云端 Widget 只对你自己选中的图片或视频做一种修改，使用的模型如下。运行时所选媒体会上传到 Glance 服务，再交给对应模型处理；Glance 不会用你的素材训练模型。

Glance has no text-to-image and no image-to-video generation. Each cloud Widget applies one edit to an image or video you selected, using the models below. The selected media is uploaded to the Glance service and processed by that model; Glance does not use your media to train models.
