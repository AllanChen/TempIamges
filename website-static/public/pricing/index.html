功能更新与续购Updates and renewal

Pro 包含 12 个月功能更新。更新期结束后，更新期内发布的版本可以一直使用。之后如果想获得新功能，可以自愿续购一年更新，价格 —，从付款当天起算，一次性付款，不会自动续费。

Pro includes 12 months of feature updates. Afterwards, every version released during that period keeps working. To get newer features you can optionally buy another year of updates for —, counted from the payment date. It is a one-time payment and never renews.

设备与离线使用Devices and offline use

同一账户可以同时在 2 台 Mac 上使用 Pro，可以自行释放旧设备（每 30 天最多 3 次）。离线时 Pro 可以继续使用 30 天，联网后自动校验。

Use Pro on 2 Macs per account at the same time, and release an old Mac yourself (up to 3 times every 30 days). Pro keeps working offline for 30 days and re-checks automatically when you reconnect.

付款方式Payment methods

付款由 Pancake by Waffo 作为记录商户（Merchant of Record）处理，并负责相关税费。人民币订单使用微信支付；美元订单可以用 Visa、Mastercard、Apple Pay、Google Pay 或微信支付。所有价格均为含税价。

Payments are processed by Pancake by Waffo as Merchant of Record, which also handles applicable taxes. CNY orders use WeChat Pay; USD orders accept Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay, Google Pay or WeChat Pay. Prices include tax.

退款Refunds

Pro 和功能更新续购在付款后 14 天内可以全额退款。点数充值包在 14 天内且完全未使用时可以全额退款。系统原因失败的 Widget 任务会自动退回点数。详见退款政策。

Pro and update renewals are fully refundable within 14 days of payment. Credit packs are fully refundable within 14 days if none of their credits have been used. Widget tasks that fail on our side refund credits automatically. See the Refund Policy.
