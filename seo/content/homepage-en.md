## View images and videos without changing your workflow

Open files from Finder, zoom into an image, browse multiple images, or inspect a video. Keep the media in front of you while you decide what needs attention.

## Compare the original with the result

Look at two images side by side, or use a comparison slider to inspect a change. Check the details before choosing which version to keep.

**Link:** Compare two images on Mac →

## Use AI tools on the media you selected

Choose a Widget from the context menu to work on an image or video. Available tools include tasks such as upscaling and background removal. Bring the result back alongside the original to check the change.

Cloud Widgets require sign-in and may use credits. The media you submit is uploaded for processing.

**Link:** Explore AI Widgets →

## Copy text from an image

Select recognized text in an image and copy it, or open the full recognition result. Text recognition runs on your Mac.

**Link:** Copy text from an image on Mac →

## Understand what is free

Viewing is free. Pro unlocks local tools through a one-time purchase. Cloud Widgets use a separate credit balance, with the cost depending on the tool.

**Link:** See current pricing →
